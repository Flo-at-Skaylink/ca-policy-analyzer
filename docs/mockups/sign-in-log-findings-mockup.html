Mockup - Sign-in log matches per policy (Policies tab)

Static, non-interactive HTML preview for review. No build/deploy involved.
Concept: when the sign-in log scan is on and completes, each policy card gets a new collapsible "Sign-in log matches" row. It surfaces real sign-ins from the last 30 days where this specific policy blocked access, or - for report-only policies - would have blocked access. Expanding shows username, timestamp, location, client app, and the failure reason straight from appliedConditionalAccessPolicies / signInEventTypes on the sign-in event.
